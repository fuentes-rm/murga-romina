## Abstract

This study investigates the influence of functionalized cationic nanocrystals on the selective flotation recovery and surface interactions of molybdenum, copper, and iron sulfide minerals (molybdenite, chalcopyrite, and pyrite), evaluating their potential as sustainable nanoreagents in sulfide mineral processing circuits.
