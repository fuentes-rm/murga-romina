My teaching experience focuses on undergraduate laboratory instruction in mining and metallurgical engineering at the University of Talca, complemented by technical workshops and scientific outreach mentorship for engineering and secondary school students.

University of Talca — Faculty of Engineering

Curicó, Chile • Undergraduate Courses (Laboratory Instructor, 2022–2024)

Flotación (Froth Flotation)

Undergraduate • Laboratory Instructor

Experimental training in sulfide and oxide mineral froth flotation, covering pulp conditioning, pH control, collector/frother/depressant dosage optimization, microflotation and batch Denver cell testing, kinetic recovery modeling, and metallurgical mass-balance evaluation.

Sulfide Mineral Flotation Collectors & Depressants Flotation Kinetics Metallurgical Accounting

Procesamiento de Minerales (Mineral Processing)

Undergraduate • Laboratory Instructor

Hands-on laboratory sessions on mineral sampling, particle size distribution analysis, crushing and grinding comminution circuits, Bond work index determination, hydrocyclone classification, solid–liquid separation, and physical concentration operations.

Comminution & Grinding Particle Size Analysis Classification Solid–Liquid Separation

Hidrometalurgia Avanzada (Advanced Hydrometallurgy)

Undergraduate • Laboratory Instructor

Experimental study of aqueous metal extraction processes, including acid and alkaline leaching kinetics, Eh–pH (Pourbaix) thermodynamic stability, solvent extraction (SX) isotherms, and electrowinning (EW) cell efficiency for copper recovery.

Leaching Kinetics Eh–pH Thermodynamics Solvent Extraction (SX) Electrowinning (EW)

Fundamentos de Metalurgia (Fundamentals of Metallurgy)

Undergraduate • Laboratory Instructor

Foundational laboratory instruction in extractive and physical metallurgy, mineralogical sample preparation, physicochemical characterization of ores and concentrates, and laboratory safety protocols in metallurgical engineering.

Extractive Metallurgy Ore Characterization Metallurgical Balances Laboratory Protocols
